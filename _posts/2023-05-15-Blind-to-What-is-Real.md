---
title: "Blind To What Is Real"

categories:
  - perspectives

tags:
  - Ecstatic
---

Driven to the cliff over the abyss.<br>
And thrust into the void like this.

Whether by slipping, leaping, or pushed,<br>
We fall.<br>
Finding refuge on a lower ledge,<br>
With a sheer wall behind rising above,<br>
We are still perched upon an edge.

We peer deeper into the abyss<br>
Mad it causes us to be.<br>
Does this mean we see less clearly?<br>
That we think less true? No!

This madness is not one of understanding.<br>
Clearer becomes our comprehension.<br>
This madness is our struggle to communicate<br>
With those who see less.

How do you connect and converse<br>
With people who avoid the abyss,<br>
Whether by choice or by privilege,<br>
Who are blind to what is real?
