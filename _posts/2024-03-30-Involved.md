title: "Involved"

Sometimes I get so involved<br>
The room around me melts<br>
The people blur, their mouths mute<br>
My thing, my focus, my need.

Much like the woman today<br>
"Can I have four dollars?"<br>
"No," as calmly and kindly as I can manage.<br>
"Can I have one dollar?"<br>
"No," with the same gentleness.<br>
"Can I have four dollars?" her voice rings down the street.

Under a bench, I see a white circle.<br>
Bending down, I find a penny caked in sidewalk salt<br>
And another and another.<br>
Feeling inspired, I note the four one-dollar bills in my pocket.

I walk down the street, patiently waiting for her current conversation to conclude.<br>
"I have four dollars."<br>
She gently takes them from me.<br>
I feel satisfied, ready to continue my day,<br>
Asking people for donations in my own way.<br>
What do I expect?<br>
Thank you maybe<br>
Perhaps she will go on her way,<br>
Having raised the required funds<br>
For food, bus fare or anything.

Not a moment has actually passed<br>
She looks into my eyes and says<br>
"Can I have some more money?"<br>
I feel the wind leave my lungs.<br>
My brow reddens.<br>
But why?

I was doing my own thing for my own reasons.<br>
So is she.<br>
Involved in her task<br>
Doing what she needs to meet her need, to survive<br>
I melt away. I am not there.<br>
I blur. There is no me to her.<br>
Not then. Not there. She is involved.
