---
title: "Pregnant Rain"

categories:
  - nature

tags:
  - Ecstatic
---


This uncertain Spring<br>
Warm sun rays<br>
Tell of carefree summer days.<br>
Clouds, wind and snow<br>
Winter is not yet ready to go.<br>
Cool mists and pregnant rains<br>
Dodging puddles and potholes wider than our lanes.

Swelling vernal ponds teaming with life<br>
Frog eggs, turtles, sunnies and newts.<br>
Spring growth from sprouting pinecones<br>
To cherry blossoming trees.<br>
Birds gathering fiber and singing a symphony for the bees.<br>
Ants peeking out to taste<br>
The sweet spring air and soil<br>
Eating that which has left this mortal coil<br>
Even chomping the bee's knees<br>
Enabling new life everywhere it please.

Thank you for coming as you are,<br>
Oh Spring come meet me, as the deer spar.<br>
Ever-present the question of what to wear,<br>
Such an uncertain and marvelous time of year.
