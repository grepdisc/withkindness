**Vote<br>**
Vote for someone you like.<br>
Vote for someone you respect.<br>
Vote for someone who represents you.<br>
Vote for someone who gets you.

Vote for someone motivated by love.<br>
Vote for someone who has empathy for you.<br>
Vote for someone who cares about people who are suffering and are living in poverty.<br>
Vote for someone who cares about equity and about the harms of growing wealth inequality.

Vote for someone who understands that we have built our nation on the bodies of our indigenous and black brethren, and that a healthy path forward begins with acceptance of this truth.

Vote for someone who recognizes that we are all in this together.
