categories:

I am a lot,<br>
Even for me.<br>
My mind runs hot.<br>
My body feels pressure,<br>
Like a fire hydrant<br>
Ready to quench an inferno.<br>
Ideas flow through my flesh,<br>
rising to a rolling boil in my brain. 

And, *psshhhhhhh!*<br>
Clouds of words<br>
Escape my mouth as a steam.<br>

I want to connect with you.<br>
I want to share and explore<br>
Feelings and ideas together.<br>
I do apologize for the burns.<br>
I am a lot,<br>
**even for me.**
