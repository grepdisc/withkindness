Be a person who loves all of yourself.<br>
Find a person who loves all of you.<br>
Love all of them.

Meet every person with love<br>
Accept each person as completely human with their infinite complexity.<br>
Be open to a future beyond your imagination.<br>

Fear not what is real.<br>
All is here<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To learn,<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To grow,<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To feel.<br>
Love and accept what is.<br>
This is opening us to receive.<br>
Try as we may, it may not be ours to perceive,<br>
But this life is not ours to leave.<br>
It is ours to lead,<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To experience,<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To love,<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To accept,<br>
And to heal.
