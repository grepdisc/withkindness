Over lunch at [Big Big Table](https://www.bigbigtable.org/),
Jesse said, “artists see what is and express it so that others can see it as well.”
Writing, like other arts, *builds up within the body until a creative explosion is ready.*
Meditation and relaxation and body awareness can help to channel
the stress and pain which may enable the body to connect with truth and creativity. 

During a time of bliss and relaxation,
where nothing is wrong and nothing has felt wrong in recent memory,
*the creative spigot may run dry.*

During times of fear and stress, which overwhelm,
*the energies that lead to creative expression may rot and go bad,*
resulting in toxins within the body and
a disappointment that an opportunity for creative expression was lost.
More immediately, the body may feel ill and depressed as a result of
*the spoilage of this creative fuel.*
