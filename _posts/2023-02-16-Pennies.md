---
title: "Pennies"

categories:
  - perspectives

tags:
  - Ecstatic
---

Walking down the sidewalk,<br>
Lost in my thoughts,<br>
Pondering whether to reach out to check on a friend today.<br>
My eye catches a spot in the dirt,<br>
Nestled among the grass<br>
Is a round outline.

Could it be?<br>
Yes, it is! It is a penny.<br>
Faded, corroded, Canadian.

I pick it up and feel so many emotions.<br>
I feel joy at having found a penny today.<br>
I feel inspiration to reach out to that friend.<br>
I feel relaxation and trust.<br>
I feel a sense that I am where I need to be,<br>
And I am doing what I need to do.<br>
I feel called to share what discovering a penny<br>
Means to me,<br>
And to express why pennies,<br>
Revealing themselves,<br>
Imbue me with confidence and happiness.
