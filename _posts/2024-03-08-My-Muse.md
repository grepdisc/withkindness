My muse lives within me,<br>
Not in a muse-zeum.<br>
My muse is not a distant bird<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To be called and welcomed as a guest.<br>
My muse is with me always<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; In my heart, in my gut, within my every skin.<br>
My muse is alive sure as day<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; When I listen deeply and care<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; When I forget myself in play<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; When I sink into prayer<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; And succumb to songs of my childhood<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; When I dance unthinking in my neighborhood<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; When I trust what will come<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Whether I sit here writing without planning<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Or I retreat into deep reflection and presence<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; In a beautiful and peaceful locale<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Like the Tenderloin of San Francisco<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Like the streets of Buffalo<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Or the stillness within my soul.<br>
My muse is <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Ever present<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Always there<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Often ignored but always ready<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To sing to me<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To sing through me<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; To sing through us
