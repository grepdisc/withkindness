**Be.<br>**
Be in this world but not of it.<br>
Be like the cat perched atop the wood pile.<br>
Be like the rat *living in the holes between labs.<br>*
Be like the dog who goes among neighbors for pats and treats.<br>
Be like the doctor, setting up clinic wherever she travels.<br>
Be like the mother who takes care of any child who comes through her door.

**Be.<br>**
*Be as light as oil on water, embracing both the air and the liquid.<br>*
Hear the pain, hear the suffering, hear the cries.<br>
Be present. Be fully with the people, emotions, and experiences.<br>
Dance, also, throughout.<br>
Move, laugh, feel, process and let go.<br>
Life is constantly rinsing and repeating.<br>
*Get your soul dirty.<br>*
Get it good and dirty with the clay of aliveness and the stains of experience.<br>
Then, dance, dance, dance to wash, cleanse and free yourself.<br>
There is another day to meet with bright-eyes to pay attention, an open-heart to share unconditional love, and a healing-ear ready to listen and care.<br>

**Be.<br>**
Be in it.<br>
*Be not caught by it.*
