---
title: "Holding Another's Heart"

categories:
  - perspectives

tags:
  - Ecstatic
---

Creating a space where one feels comfortable<br>
To open their chest<br>
To remove their beating heart<br>
To cradle it in their hands<br>
Mingled with your hands.

Holding another's heart<br>
Calls for such complete presence.<br>
It brings *ecstatic euphoria*.<br>
It can exhaust us in ways we have not imagined.

It is the best experience,<br>
Coalescing into memories<br>
That, touched by the divine, bring resilience<br>
And confidence in who we are<br>
And in *whom we can each be with each other*.
