**Sharing an Idea**

An idea *encased in a body*<br>
becomes accessible<br>
as ink touches paper or as voice forms words.

Like breath,<br>
visible briefly in chilled air,<br>
*words reveal aspects of an idea*<br>
as it begins to decay immediately<br>
upon departing its fleshly home.
