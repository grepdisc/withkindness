title: "Living Opportunity"

categories:

tags:

Staring into the abyss<br>
I start to fall forward down.<br>
Branches I hit, ledges too.<br>
Obstacles race toward me<br>
I throw my momentum left<br>
I miss a ledge, that's a success.<br>
*Falling is so so scarring.*

Like skiing down a steep slope<br>
Trees, cliffs, bumps, a life-changing<br>
Tumble or crack await me soon.

Like driving down the freeway<br>
Freefall with danger all around<br>
Pressing the brake, feeling sponge.<br>

Like pouring sand of savings<br>
Onto my financial castle,<br>
Watching ocean's constant waves<br>
Washing my future beach flat.<br>

*Each day is a brand new gift.*<br>
Presence in this moment now<br>
Living in the flow with all<br>
Dancing, prancing and driving<br>
Surrendering all control<br>
To feel fully in control<br>
Again, building and creating<br>
Living opportunity.

**Look away from the abyss.**<br>
*You have seen enough for now.*<br>
Now, gaze into loving eyes.<br>
Your beloveds want to grow<br>
In the flow with you always.<br>
*Like wildflowers in the field*<br>
Nothing will ever stop us.
