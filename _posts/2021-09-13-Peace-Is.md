Peace is not an absence.<br>
Peace is a presence:<br>
a presence of quiet,<br>
Safety,<br>
Community,<br>
Connection,<br>
Love,<br>
Shared joy,<br>
Shared hope,<br>
Shared communication,<br>
And shared understanding.<br>
Peace is a presence of overlapping realities.<br>

Peace is much more than an absence of violence.
