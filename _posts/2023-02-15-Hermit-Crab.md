---
title: "Walk Like The Hermit Crab"

categories:

tags:
  - Ecstatic
---


Like sand crabs,<br>
Frantically scrambling across the beach,<br>
Moving from hole to hole,<br>
Escaping the rolling surf,<br>
The soaring birds,<br>
Any threat really.

So, we too move from secure situation to secure situation.<br>
A home, a relationship, a job.<br>
Each of these are our holes.

Our house may fall apart,<br>
roaches, lack of plumbing or electricity,<br>
violent neighbors or family.<br>
But anything is better than no home, right?

Our relationships can maintain their bonds,<br>
their hold over us,<br>
as they go from bad to worse to unimaginable.<br>
Being tracked, controlled, isolated, terrified,<br>
and yet this is better than being alone, right?<br>
If only someone would tell us when it is over,<br>
but only we can tell ourselves that, right?

A job gives us purpose, status and resources to live freely.<br>
We get what we need from a job<br>
and it is a vehicle for our participation and contributions to social systems.<br>
A job is a negotiation of what others need<br>
and what we want to give,<br>
and how much we give<br>
and how much we get.<br>
When the deal becomes unbalanced,<br>
*a job becomes a hole in which a rising sea imperceptibly drowns us.*

Why do we need to frantically strafe the sand?<br>
Why not walk like the hermit crab?<br>
Carrying our security on our back,<br>
we can carefully inspect that next home, relationship, job.<br>
We can accept or discard it as we choose.

*Oh for the courage and confidence of that hermit crab,<br>
rolling in the ocean surf,<br>
knowing we will be okay wherever we go.*
