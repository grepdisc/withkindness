categories:

Let go!<br>
Let go the tension.<br>
Let go the nervous energy.<br>
Let go the worry.<br>
We are safe.<br>
This moment is for peace<br>
For rest.

Let our cares dissolve into euphoria.<br>
Now is ours to disappear.<br>
Our action now is restoration:<br>
Sleep, a blissful destination,<br>
Community, hugs, love,<br>
Unconditional love from others<>
And for ourselves.<br>
Others are here to share<br>
Loving energy shown us with care.

Rest, rest, rest.<br>
Feel our muscles loosen.<br>
Notice our breath calm.<br>
Let our lids close,<br>
Knowing our world will wait<br>
Until whenever we return.<br>
Let us forget all<br>
But joy and affection.

Let's stay here. <br>
There's nothing to control.<br>
We can just be.<br>
Be, be, breathe.<br>
Breathe in and be.

We will be ready.<br>
We will remember.<br>
We will rediscover our strength and stamina.<br>
We will know<br>
When we hear our body shouting<br>
Let's go.<br>
Let's go!
