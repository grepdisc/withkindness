*Written at [WRITE ON!](https://kindfools.org/writeon/) on Feb 21, 2023*<br><br>

What more can I aspire to embody than grace?<br>
I love this so so much!<br>
I have already forgiven myself<br>
For leaving that reflection on grace<br>
To pine away alone,<br>
As my mind fills with enthusiasm and joy sitting here.

It soothes me to see pens moving and<br>
People focused on pouring their hearts,<br>
Souls, and dreams onto paper.<br>
Sitting in this circle,<br>
I feel myself filled up by others<br>
Following a process that has poetrified me.

By surrendering oneself<br>
Into a space of sitting and writing together,<br>
One is able to walk down the bank<br>
And dip into the river that has filled to overflowing<br>
With our collective creativity.

Feeling inspiration channel through me,<br>
As I see it flowing through so many others,<br>
Makes me think of tributaries,<br>
Each gathering rain and snow melt<br>
In forests and in Buffalo,<br>
Growing and flooding into torrents of creativity<br>
That dwarfs even the falls of Niagara.

To tell the truth.<br>
To tell the truth.<br>
Thank you for sharing with me this time,<br>
This space,<br>
This night-time,<br>
This headspace,<br>
This grace.
