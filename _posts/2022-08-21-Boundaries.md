Boundaries, they help us to color within the lines,
to paint delicate visions that approach and even embrace each other,
while maintaining distinct edges.

We pour from our cups. **Boundaries are the walls of our cups.**
Without boundaries, we are not pouring from an empty cup.
No, *we are spilling from a plate or a coaster.* We cannot pour.

Boundaries help us to assert ourselves, to express ourselves.
When we are in a relationship with another,
it is their boundaries that express who they are.
"I love you" can be reframed as, "I love your boundaries."
*My boundaries love your boundaries.
They feel cozy and safe next to each other,
embracing and maintaining.*
Your boundaries and my boundaries love each other so much
that we want to build "our boundaries" to express our love into this world.
