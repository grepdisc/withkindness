categories:

tags:

I am a person.<br>
I am proud of my accomplishments.<br>
I have regrets for actions I've taken<br>
And for ones I might yet take.<br>
I love my friends.<br>
I am grateful for their support.<br>
Put not your expectations upon me.<br>
They are not mine to carry.

Inspired by my wise playful friend Edward Tyler 1960-2018.
