I sit here with my tiny brain<br>
Trying to comprehend all of this domain.<br>
Who am I to inflict my clumsy understanding on anyone, even myself?<br>
The spectre of doubt rises in me<br>
As a plea to stop my practice,<br>
To shame me into submission. <br>
Am I a coward, who will prolong this quest of despair?<br>
Or, will I find the courage to cease my hubris and be.

To tell the truth<br>
I have no idea what will flow<br>
I want to rhyme, that I know.<br>
A dark cloud spreads over me.<br>
The sun dims, obscured is she.<br>
A rainbow I see, looking backward,<br>
But a dark hard future forward.

This too will choo choo choo away<br>
Like a train, bursting from tunnel into day.<br>
I know so much that I don’t know<br>
But one truth that is always so:<br>
The next moment, day, year may hold<br>
A feeling, experience, reality so bold<br>
That my little brain could never imagine or dream<br>
That fills my heart with love and lights that beam.
