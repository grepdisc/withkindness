title: "Hello Brightness"

categories:

tags:

Hello brightness, my old friend,<br>
Your presence is sure to mend.<br>
After a long dark winter,<br>
You glisten on the vestigial snow. 

Better days are here.<br>
This is a new spring,<br>
Not for forever but for now. 

All ideas feel actionable. <br>
Unshackled possibilities expand before us.<br>
Each path looks well lit and relatively flat,<br>
From this distance.

On which path shall we walk?

Oh, what a fun question!

Let's take this moment to enjoy.<br>
The doubts are quiet.<br>
The patterns are shifting in their crumbling molds.<br>
We have so many toys and skills.<br>
**What fun** to bring them together<br>
To play and build and define this nascent reality. 

**What fun** to breathe into<br>
A more human way of living. 

**What fun** to release the burden<br>
Of large hierarchies that drain us,<br>
That set us to work helping people<br>
Only by extracting our own life force<br>
Until what was our dreams<br>
Are nothing but dim gray memes. 

**What fun** to move toward<br>
Community-based nimble groups. <br>
We sustain ourselves and each other by meeting needs.<br>
Needs of resources, healing and purpose. <br>

Cohesive groups, independently guided and interdependent,<br>
Feeling familiarly inclusive, welcoming and nurturing. 

We seek to recognize and dismantle bias and oppressions<br>
Within both ourselves and our groups.<br>
And so, to serve as examples of how to walk a path<br>
Toward a society with systems and institutions<br>
That have either repaired their crumbling foundations<br>
Of racism, sexism, able-ism and such,<br>
Or, been reconstructed by their composing communities<br>
Without these structural keystones of sand.
