**Life is a dance<br>**
With each song,<br>
we get a little bit stronger.<br>
We make friends.<br>
We copy their moves.<br>
*We keep those we like.<br>*
We make them our own.

Some of our dance partners have more moves.<br>
Some have been dancing longer.<br>
*Some will leave the dance before us.<br>*
Some will dance long after we’ve danced to our final song.

The songs will keep coming.<br>
The dancers will keep coming.

The questions are:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;What moves have I tried?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Whose moves will I dance forward?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Which moves will I pass on and to whom?*
