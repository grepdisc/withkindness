Listening to others' dreams lights me up.<br>
I shine as we explore lighting our way<br>
by them living into their dreams.

As we share our dreams,<br>
entrusting each other with our own divine revelations,<br>
perhaps our dreams may overlap more and more<br>
with each other<br>
and with this perception we call reality.
