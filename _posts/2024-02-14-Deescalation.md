Peace<br>
Peace within me<br>
Inspires peace within you.<br>
Respect and care – that is all.

Space<br>
Space within my heart<br>
Helps me to listen to you.<br>
Space around you<br>
Helps you to feel safe and respected.<br>
Space around me<br>
Keeps me safe and calm.

Know where the exits are.<br>
You need an exit just as much as me.

Consent is everything.<br>
I can offer, but I must not give<br>
Unless my offer is accepted.

Backing away from you is a rejection.<br>
It’s okay that you feel that,<br>
But I also need to maintain space<br>
So that I can feel safe,<br>
And so I can be here with you.

“Thank you,”<br>
These words mean more than we can imagine.<br>
“I hear you.”<br>
“That sucks.”<br>
“Wow, I had no idea, I’m sorry.”<br>
“We are all in this together.”<br>
“Good to see you today.”<br>
“I look forward to seeing you again tomorrow.”
