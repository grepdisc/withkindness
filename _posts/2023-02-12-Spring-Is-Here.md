I am grateful to the Buffalo Ecstatic Dance community, and to the dancers who inspired this reflection today, February 12, 2023.


**The first day of spring.<br>**
Dancing outside on this dry sunny day.<br>
The trees display their winter barreness.<br>
The grass is yellow as the Earth braces for more frost,<br>
but dancing outside by the warmth of the sun and the fire,<br>
and breathing in the clear air,<br>
I feel that spring is here.<br>

New life.<br>
Rebirth.<br>
Growth and things positive beyond my imagination are imminent.<br>
*Trust. Breathe. Smell. Laugh.*

Amidst a sea of smiling peeps,<br>
Each dancing to their own beat,<br>
Each experiencing their own joy,<br>
Each grateful and happier for this community,<br>
Each immersed in ecstatic energy.

We infect each other.
We amplify, inspire and escalate the scene.<br>
We each strive to feel, be and play more authentically.<br>
It is not a competition.<br>
*It is the point.*
