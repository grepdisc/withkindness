A team notices each others’ strengths and becomes aware of an individual’s limitations.<br>
A team fosters each player’s growth and feeds each person’s living spirit.<br>
A team is measured by what we achieve together, and by whether we all get there.

A team pays attention with many eyes, and ears and hearts.<br>
A team is nurturing and inspiring.<br>
A team is solidarity.
