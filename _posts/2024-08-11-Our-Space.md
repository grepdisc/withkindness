Hold my space
<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When I’m here
<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When I’m gone<br>
There is no line<br>
There is always<br>
Us, each other

Alone with my thoughts<br>
I am scared<br>
Not good enough<br>
Not good<br>
No hope

Listening to you<br>
I feel… better<br>
I am here, with you<br>
I am here, for you<br>
You fear, what I fear<br>
I feel seen, in your words

Nothing has changed<br>
For me,<br>
Everything has changed
<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In your words
<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In your ears
<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In your eyes<br>
I can move again

Fear is out There<br>
But we are not<br>
In this sacred space<br>
We are already Here.
